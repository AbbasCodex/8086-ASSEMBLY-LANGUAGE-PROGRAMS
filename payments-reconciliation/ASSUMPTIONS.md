# Assumptions

1. **A1 — Scope and reporting periods.** The reproducible dataset contains January and February 2025 transaction cohorts. Each cohort is reconciled at its own calendar month-end; settlements received after that cohort's cutoff are shown as timing items, not losses.
2. **A2 — Timezone and cutoff.** Timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC. A month closes at 23:59:59.999999 UTC on its last calendar day.
3. **A3 — Currency.** Every generated row is USD. Currency is still carried on every row and is part of every match and aggregate key. Amounts are represented as decimal strings; ordinary rows have cent precision and the planted rounding batch has three-decimal precision.
4. **A4 — Amount and sign conventions.** `amount` is positive gross transaction value. Capture `net_amount` is gross less a nonnegative fee. Refund `net_amount` is negative; its `amount` and `fee` are positive magnitudes. Reconciliation totals use signed net amounts.
5. **A5 — Fees.** Fees are included in `net_amount`; there is no separate bank fee deduction beyond the row's stated fee. Platform and bank `net_amount` are compared, not gross `amount`.
6. **A6 — Settlement lag.** Ordinary captures settle one or two calendar days after creation. Generated ordinary dates avoid month-end so exactly one intentionally planted capture settles in the following month. A real production implementation must estimate lag from observed processor behavior.
7. **A7 — Match priority.** A unique `bank_ref` match is attempted first. Remaining rows may fuzzy-match only when customer, currency, and created/settled dates are compatible and cent-rounded net amounts differ by no more than one cent. A tolerated one-cent delta is recorded, not silently discarded.
8. **A8 — Date window.** Fuzzy matching permits settlement from the creation date through two calendar days after it, including a following-month settlement. A row outside this window is not a fuzzy match.
9. **A9 — Duplicates.** A repeated `txn_id` is a duplicate only when all business fields except `row_id` are identical; the lexicographically first row ID is canonical and subsequent identical rows are duplicate submissions. Same-ID rows with different values are retained as separate records and classified as amount mismatches for review, not silently discarded.
10. **A10 — Refunds.** A refund is valid only if a capture with the referenced `original_txn_id` exists in the platform file. Refunds are not required to equal the original amount, so partial refunds are allowed. A refund with no original capture is classified `ORPHAN_REFUND` before ordinary matching.
11. **A11 — Settlement batches.** A batch is identified by `settlement_batch_id`, currency, and settlement month. Platform rows carry the expected bank batch; bank rows carry the actual batch. Aggregate rounding checks compare source net sums for the same key and report only a nonzero cent-rounded residual after row-level cent comparisons match.
12. **A12 — Reporting totals.** Platform totals include all platform rows created in the cohort month; bank totals include only bank rows settled by that cohort's month-end and associated with that cohort. Totals are summed at source precision and rounded to cents. A settlement after cutoff is therefore an explained positive platform-minus-bank timing delta.
13. **A13 — Variance equation.** The signed unexplained residual is `platform_total - bank_total - sum(explained_deltas) - rounding_residual`. Explained deltas use platform-minus-bank sign convention: missing bank value is positive, bank-only value is negative, extra platform value is positive, and extra bank value is negative.
14. **A14 — Ground truth.** The generator writes a seeded manifest that identifies the planted row IDs and each control. Naturally occurring cohort/batch timing is minimized by construction; any additional timing is reported rather than suppressed.
15. **A15 — Tolerance control.** The intentional within-tolerance control differs by one cent in net amount and remains `MATCHED`; its signed delta is retained in the machine-readable output and conservation equation.
16. **A16 — Clean control.** Tests use a separate exact-match month with no planted anomalies to verify zero unexplained delta. The generated February cohort is not assumed to be clean because its planted events are part of the shared dataset.
