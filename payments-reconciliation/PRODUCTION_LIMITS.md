This deterministic demo would misstate production exposure when partial captures, reversals, or partial refunds do not map one-to-one to transaction IDs. It assumes one currency and explicit source net amounts, so it would not correctly reconstruct settlement-time FX conversion or processor-specific gross-versus-net fee treatment. Its duplicate heuristic treats repeated transaction IDs as retries even though legitimate split payments or processor re-submissions can share identifiers, and fuzzy matching may therefore require human review.
